# LifeWave app — brand assets & provenance (ASSETS.md)

Retrieval date for every asset below: **2026-10-17**. All assets were fetched by plain HTTP (curl) from official LifeWave public pages / the official LifeWave CDN (`cdn.sanity.io`, the CMS CDN that serves lifewave.com — the site itself preconnects to it and loads all product imagery from it). No images were taken from Facebook groups, distributor pages, or third-party sites. Product shots and the logo only — no testimonial imagery, no people/results photos.

Bundled files live in `assets/brand/`; the machine-readable map is `assets/assets.json`.
Total size of `assets/brand/`: **224,568 bytes (~0.21 MB)** — well under the ~5 MB budget (BRANDING.md / SPEC §7.12).
Product thumbnails: WebP, longest side ≤ 600 px, converted with Python Pillow from the official originals. Every output file was verified as a real image (`file` reports RIFF/WebP for each; Pillow re-opened and verified all 25) and visually spot-checked on a contact sheet against the product names.
